12 / TIMELINE

TimelineRuler

import { TimelineRuler } from '@karoly-pfaff/audio-controls/timeline'

A source-time ruler with deterministic ticks and compact clock labels. As zoom increases, it creates more ticks across the full content width.

Props

duration is positive source seconds; zoom is the multiplier from TimelineViewport. The different prop name is intentional: it is duration here, whereas most other timeline controls use durationSeconds.

At zoom 1, the ruler normally divides a source into six intervals. Subsecond intervals include centiseconds. Tick density is bounded by the source duration and the clamped zoom, not an independently chosen BPM or beat grid.

For your own ruler rendering, use timelineTicks and retain the same source-time percentage positions.
